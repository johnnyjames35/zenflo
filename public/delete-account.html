Delete your ZenFlo account

Delete inside the app

Sign in to ZenFlo, select Delete account at the top of the app, enter your password and confirm. Your account and associated Brain Dumps, tasks, check-ins and session data will be permanently deleted.

Request deletion without app access

Email hello@zenflo.co.uk from the address registered to your ZenFlo account with the subject “ZenFlo account deletion request”. We may ask you to verify ownership before deletion.

Payment records that must be retained for tax, fraud-prevention or legal obligations may be kept only for the required period.
