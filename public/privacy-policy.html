Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

ZenFlo helps you manage ADHD at work, so some of what you log here is personal. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it's protected.

1. Who We Are

ZenFlo is operated by Cambrian Digital (Big Bulldog UK Ltd), based in Wales, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at hello@zenflo.co.uk.

2. Information We Collect

To provide the ZenFlo service, we collect:

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored securely, never in plain text)
  • Content you create: Brain Dumps, task breakdowns, daily check-ins, and any notes or entries you add while using the app
  • Payment information: Pro subscription payments are handled entirely by Stripe — we do not see or store your card details
  • Technical data: standard information such as IP address and browser type

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain your ZenFlo account and the features you use (Brain Dump, Task Breakdown, Focus Timer, Daily Check-In, Workplace Scripts, Wind Down)
  • Process your Pro subscription and send payment-related emails via Stripe
  • Send you account-related emails, such as welcome messages or password resets
  • Improve the app and understand how it's used

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with the third parties needed to run ZenFlo:

  • Stripe — payment processing for Pro subscriptions
  • Railway — hosting and database infrastructure

We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to request a copy of the information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@zenflo.co.uk.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain records for legal or accounting purposes.

8. Cookies

ZenFlo uses only essential cookies needed to keep you signed in and to remember your session. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at hello@zenflo.co.uk.
